Daily record

How scoring works

Each trading day the tool snapshots its own morning recommendation (verdict, conviction, top warrant, watch levels, prices). After the 16:00 HK close it scores itself out of 10:
Direction (0–4): did the day close in the called direction? Full marks for a clear move the right way; partial for flat days; zero for wrong-way. NO-TRADE calls earn marks when the day was indeed range-bound (<0.5%).
Warrant (0–3): did the recommended warrant's bid actually gain? ≥5% = 3, positive = 2, flat = 1, loss = 0. Uses live end-of-day bid when available, sensitivity-model estimate otherwise (flagged est).
Levels (0–3): how close did the actual high/low come to the predicted watch levels? Within 0.4% = 3 · 0.8% = 2 · 1.5% = 1.
Overall % = average daily score ÷ 10, weighted by conviction (a high-conviction miss hurts more than a tentative one). Paper P&L simulates RM5,000 into each day's pick at the morning offer, out at the closing bid — the most honest single metric. Days when the app wasn't opened before close are skipped, not guessed.
